A roguelite solitaire for iPhone

Slay the court.
Take the crown.

Usurper is solitaire played with a standard deck and a grudge. Bring down knaves, queens and kings with three cards at a time, descend nine floors beneath the palace, and topple three thrones on the way to the Usurper himself.

  • Three cards to a slaying. Two attackers that add up to the royal's power, then a seal in its suit.
  • Might × Fury = Glory. Perfect strikes, kinslayings and chains stack Fury for a big finish on every kill.
  • Your deck is your health. Burn a card to escape a bad hand, but every burn is a wound.
  • The Black Market. Recruit, buy regalia and curios, enchant and burn cards between floors.
  • Three throne rooms. Each boss imposes a Decree that bends the rules.

Usurper is in beta on TestFlight.
