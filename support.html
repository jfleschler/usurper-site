Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email jon.fleschler@gmail.com with your device and what happened. Screenshots help.

Beta feedback

Playing the TestFlight beta? Take a screenshot in the game and TestFlight offers to send it as feedback, or open the TestFlight app and tap Send Beta Feedback. Crashes are reported automatically.

How a slaying works

Play two cards on a royal whose values add up to at least its power: 11 for a knave, 12 for a queen, 13 for a king. Then play a third card in the royal's suit to seal it. The chip on each royal shows what it needs next. How to Play on the title screen walks through it with pictures.

Using your whole hand

You draw three cards and must play them all. A card that fits nowhere can go in the Satchel to save for later, or on the Pyre at the cost of a wound. At eight wounds you fall. Every slaying heals one.

Stuck?

Tap the ? medallion beside the Pyre and the game suggests a move. Tap any royal, regalia or curio to read what it does.

Starting over

To give up a run, choose Abandon Run in the pause menu, or New Run on the title screen. The pause menu and Settings on the title screen turn music, sound and haptics on or off and set the animation speed.
